Our Priority Issues

James Okello's campaign focuses on the issues that matter most to Ugandan families and communities.

🎓

Education Reform

Improving access to quality education, teacher training, and educational infrastructure across all districts.

❤️

Healthcare Access

Expanding healthcare facilities, medical supplies, and health insurance coverage for all Ugandans.

🌱

Agricultural Development

Supporting farmers with modern techniques, fair market access, and sustainable farming practices.

💼

Job Creation

Creating employment opportunities through skills training, small business support, and economic development.

🏗️

Infrastructure

Building better roads, improving electricity access, and expanding internet connectivity nationwide.

⚖️

Good Governance

Promoting transparency, accountability, and citizen participation in government decision-making.
