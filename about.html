About James Okello

A lifelong advocate for the people of Uganda, dedicated to building stronger communities and creating opportunities for all.

Early Life & Education

Born in Gulu District in 1978, James Okello grew up in a farming family that taught him the values of hard work, community service, and education. Despite financial challenges, his parents prioritized his education, instilling in him a deep appreciation for learning and the transformative power of knowledge.

James excelled academically, earning a scholarship to study at Makerere University where he obtained his Bachelor's degree in Social Sciences and later pursued a Master's in Public Administration. During his university years, he was actively involved in student leadership and community outreach programs.

Career & Public Service

After graduation, James began his career as a community development officer, working directly with rural communities to implement development projects. His success in improving education access and healthcare delivery in remote areas earned him recognition and eventually led to his election as District Councilor for Gulu District in 2016.

During his tenure as District Councilor, James championed several landmark initiatives including the Rural Education Enhancement Program, which increased school enrollment by 40%, and the Community Health Worker Initiative, which improved healthcare access in underserved areas.

Personal Life & Values

James is married to Dr. Sarah Okello, a physician who shares his passion for community service. Together, they have three children and remain deeply rooted in their community. James believes that effective leadership starts with understanding the daily challenges faced by ordinary citizens.
