Recent Campaign Highlights

📅 December 1, 2024

Massive Turnout at Lira Rally

Over 15,000 supporters gathered in Lira to hear James Okello's message of unity and development. The event featured discussions on agricultural modernization and youth employment, with strong community participation and enthusiasm.

📍 Lira Municipal Stadium 👥 15,000+ Attendees
📅 November 28, 2024

School Construction Project Launch

James Okello officially launched the construction of a new primary school in Karamoja region, demonstrating his commitment to education access. The project will benefit over 800 children in the surrounding communities.

📍 Karamoja Region 👥 800+ Students Benefited
📅 November 25, 2024

Free Healthcare Camp in Busoga

Organized a comprehensive healthcare camp providing free medical services, health education, and medication to underserved communities in Busoga region. Over 2,000 people received care during the three-day event.

📍 Busoga Region 👥 2,000+ People Served
